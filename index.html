安装Install

不确定芯片类型？打开「苹果菜单 › 关于本机」，看“芯片”或“处理器”一栏。Not sure which chip you have? Open › About This Mac and check “Chip” or “Processor”.

  1. 下载与本机芯片匹配的 DMG，打开后把 TickKey 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。Download the DMG for your chip, open it and drag TickKey into Applications.
  2. 从「应用程序」中打开 TickKey，扫码、拖入二维码图片或导入备份即可添加账户。Launch TickKey from Applications, then add accounts by dropping in QR images or importing a backup.
  3. 之后的新版本通过内置的 Sparkle 自动更新获取，也可在设置中手动检查。Future versions arrive through the built-in Sparkle updater, or check manually in Settings.
  4. iOS 版暂未上架 App Store；Release 中的 iOS-AdHoc.ipa 只能安装到已加入描述文件的设备。The iOS app is not on the App Store yet. The iOS-AdHoc.ipa in each release installs only on provisioned devices.
